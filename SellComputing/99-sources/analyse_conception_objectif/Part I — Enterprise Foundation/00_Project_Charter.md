# Project Atlas

## Project Charter

**Version :** 1.0
**Status :** Draft
**Classification :** Internal
**Architecture Framework :** TOGAF ADM
**Phase :** Foundation (Phase 0)

---

# 1. Purpose

This document formally establishes the initiation of **Project Atlas**.

It defines the strategic intent, governance boundaries, high-level objectives and guiding principles that will govern every architectural and business decision throughout the project lifecycle.

The Project Charter does **not** define the solution.

Instead, it authorizes the architectural work required to discover, validate and design the future enterprise.

---

# 2. Background

The rapid growth of computing technologies has created an increasingly complex purchasing environment.

Consumers, students, professionals and organizations are confronted with an overwhelming number of hardware options, technical specifications, brands and compatibility constraints.

Most purchasing decisions are therefore based on incomplete information, marketing influence or fragmented advice.

Existing e-commerce platforms primarily optimize product distribution rather than decision quality.

Project Atlas originates from the observation that purchasing computing equipment should begin with understanding user needs rather than browsing product catalogs.

---

# 3. Project Vision

Project Atlas aims to establish a trusted digital ecosystem dedicated to supporting informed computing decisions throughout the complete lifecycle of digital equipment.

Rather than positioning itself solely as an online retailer, the initiative seeks to become a long-term reference for guidance, knowledge, services and technology acquisition.

Commerce represents one capability of the ecosystem rather than its primary identity.

---

# 4. Mission

Design and build an enterprise capable of assisting individuals and organizations in selecting, acquiring, maintaining and evolving computing equipment through trusted expertise, structured knowledge and integrated digital services.

---

# 5. Strategic Objectives

The project shall pursue the following strategic objectives:

* Improve the quality of technology purchasing decisions.
* Reduce uncertainty before acquisition.
* Democratize access to understandable technical knowledge.
* Create a trusted relationship between users and digital technologies.
* Build a scalable ecosystem of complementary services.
* Establish a sustainable business model centered on long-term customer value rather than one-time transactions.

---

# 6. Scope

The Project Charter authorizes the architectural definition of:

* Business architecture.
* Information architecture.
* Application architecture.
* Technology architecture.
* Governance model.
* Operating model.
* Digital service ecosystem.
* Future implementation roadmap.

The following elements are explicitly outside the scope of this document:

* Software design.
* User interface design.
* Technical implementation.
* Programming languages.
* Infrastructure selection.
* Commercial partnerships.
* Product catalog definition.

---

# 7. Expected Outcomes

The project is expected to deliver:

* A complete enterprise architecture.
* A coherent digital platform strategy.
* A scalable business operating model.
* A unified information model.
* A reusable technology architecture.
* A phased implementation roadmap.
* Governance mechanisms supporting long-term evolution.

---

# 8. Success Criteria

Project Atlas will be considered successful if it enables the creation of an enterprise capable of:

* delivering measurable value to its users;
* adapting continuously to technological evolution;
* maintaining architectural consistency over time;
* supporting future service expansion without fundamental redesign;
* establishing long-term trust with customers and partners.

---

# 9. Assumptions

The project is based on the following initial assumptions:

* Computing technologies will continue to evolve rapidly.
* Decision complexity will continue to increase.
* Users increasingly expect personalized digital experiences.
* Reliable information constitutes a strategic competitive advantage.
* Digital services extend beyond traditional e-commerce.

These assumptions will be validated during subsequent architecture phases.

---

# 10. Constraints

At this stage, the following constraints are recognized:

* Architectural decisions shall remain technology independent whenever possible.
* Business requirements shall drive technical decisions.
* The enterprise architecture shall prioritize maintainability, scalability and interoperability.
* Every major architectural decision shall be documented and justified.

---

# 11. Governance Principles

The project shall be governed according to the following principles:

1. Business before technology.
2. Architecture before implementation.
3. Data as a strategic asset.
4. Service-oriented thinking.
5. User trust as a primary objective.
6. Incremental and evolutive architecture.
7. Documentation-driven decision making.

---

# 12. Deliverables of Phase 0

Phase 0 will produce the foundational architecture documentation required before entering the TOGAF ADM lifecycle.

The expected deliverables include:

* Problem Statement
* Vision
* Mission
* Enterprise Principles
* Value Proposition
* Stakeholder Map
* Business Context
* Assumptions and Constraints
* Glossary

---

# 13. Approval

Approval of this Project Charter authorizes the enterprise architecture work required to formally define Project Atlas.

It does not authorize implementation activities.

Implementation may only begin after completion of the architectural phases and formal governance approval.
