# 1. Purpose

This document defines the enterprise decision model of Project Atlas.

It establishes the common method by which the enterprise transforms user requests into informed, contextualized and explainable computing recommendations.

The decision model is technology-independent and applies uniformly to human advisors, digital services and future intelligent systems.

---

# 2. Decision Philosophy

Project Atlas does not sell products based solely on expressed requests.

It supports users in making informed decisions by understanding their real needs, constraints and objectives.

The enterprise enables decisions.

It does not make decisions on behalf of users.

---

# 3. Decision Transformation Model

Every decision follows the same conceptual process.

```text
User Request
        ↓
Request Interpretation
        ↓
Need Identification
        ↓
Context Analysis
        ↓
Constraint Analysis
        ↓
Knowledge Exploration
        ↓
Scenario Generation
        ↓
Scenario Evaluation
        ↓
Explainable Recommendation
        ↓
User Decision
        ↓
Experience Feedback
        ↓
Knowledge Enrichment
```

---

# 4. Decision Inputs

A recommendation shall consider, whenever relevant:

## User Profile

* education
* profession
* technical level
* accessibility needs

## Intended Usage

* office work
* software development
* gaming
* CAD / 3D
* multimedia
* artificial intelligence
* education
* business

## Constraints

* budget
* availability
* mobility
* autonomy
* durability
* repairability
* preferred condition (new or refurbished)

## Local Context

* availability of spare parts
* availability of maintenance services
* electrical conditions
* internet connectivity
* regional market conditions

## Technical Knowledge

* product specifications
* benchmarks
* compatibility
* technology generations
* known limitations
* upgrade possibilities

---

# 5. Decision Principles

Every recommendation shall satisfy the following principles.

### Need Before Product

Recommendations begin with understanding needs rather than selecting products.

### Context Before Specification

Technical specifications are interpreted within the user's context.

### Multiple Scenarios

The enterprise proposes justified alternatives whenever appropriate.

### Explainability

Every recommendation includes the reasoning supporting it.

### Transparency

Assumptions, limitations and trade-offs are explicitly communicated.

### Continuous Learning

Every validated experience contributes to improving future recommendations.

---

# 6. Recommendation Structure

Every recommendation shall contain:

* identified need;
* assumptions;
* considered constraints;
* evaluated scenarios;
* recommended option(s);
* justification;
* identified compromises;
* possible future evolution.

---

# 7. Explainability Model

Recommendations shall answer at least the following questions:

* Why this recommendation?
* Why not another option?
* Which constraints influenced the result?
* Which assumptions were made?
* What are the trade-offs?
* How can the solution evolve over time?

---

# 8. Enterprise Learning Loop

Every completed customer journey generates new enterprise knowledge.

Possible learning sources include:

* customer satisfaction;
* equipment reliability;
* maintenance history;
* upgrade history;
* recurring questions;
* purchasing trends;
* community discussions;
* expert reviews.

Validated insights shall enrich the enterprise knowledge base and improve future decision quality.

---

# 9. Decision Quality

Decision quality is evaluated according to:

* relevance to the user's real needs;
* technical accuracy;
* transparency;
* explainability;
* long-term suitability;
* customer confidence;
* post-purchase satisfaction.

Commercial success alone is not considered a sufficient indicator of decision quality.

---

# 10. Architectural Implications

The enterprise decision model requires:

* a structured knowledge graph;
* traceable decision criteria;
* reusable decision rules;
* explainable recommendation mechanisms;
* continuous knowledge enrichment;
* integration between knowledge, community and commerce.

The decision model constitutes a strategic enterprise asset and shall remain independent of any specific implementation technology.
