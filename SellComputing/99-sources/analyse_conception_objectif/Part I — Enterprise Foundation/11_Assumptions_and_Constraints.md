# Project Atlas

# Assumptions and Constraints

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Classification:** Internal
**Architecture Framework:** TOGAF ADM (Extension)
**Phase:** Foundation (Phase 0)

---

# 1. Purpose

This document records the strategic assumptions and architectural constraints that influence the design, governance and long-term evolution of Project Atlas.

These assumptions shall be periodically validated throughout the enterprise architecture lifecycle.

Constraints are considered mandatory design requirements.

---

# 2. Strategic Assumptions

The enterprise assumes that:

* users seek trusted guidance rather than product listings alone;
* explainable recommendations increase confidence;
* community participation enriches enterprise knowledge;
* educational content improves long-term customer relationships;
* structured knowledge becomes a sustainable competitive advantage;
* localized decision support creates greater value than generic recommendations.

---

# 3. Business Constraints

Project Atlas shall:

* prioritize understanding before recommending;
* support the complete equipment lifecycle;
* preserve long-term customer trust;
* integrate knowledge, community and commerce within a unified enterprise model.

---

# 4. Knowledge Constraints

Enterprise knowledge shall:

* never exist in isolation;
* remain interconnected through the Enterprise Knowledge Fabric;
* maintain traceable origins;
* support versioning and continuous enrichment;
* preserve historical value whenever relevant.

Knowledge preservation is considered a strategic enterprise responsibility.

---

# 5. Community Constraints

Community interactions shall:

* remain attached to knowledge assets;
* contribute to collective understanding;
* support constructive collaboration;
* preserve valuable technical discussions for future reuse.

---

# 6. Decision Constraints

Recommendations shall always be:

* contextual;
* explainable;
* transparent;
* evidence-based;
* traceable.

Commercial objectives shall never override recommendation integrity.

---

# 7. Technical Constraints

The enterprise architecture shall:

* remain modular;
* follow open standards whenever practical;
* support interoperability;
* enable progressive evolution;
* minimize vendor lock-in.

Technology choices shall remain subordinate to business architecture.

---

# 8. Governance Constraints

Enterprise governance shall ensure:

* conceptual consistency;
* knowledge quality;
* architectural compliance;
* continuous validation of strategic assumptions;
* controlled enterprise evolution.

---

# 9. Evolution Constraints

Every future capability shall:

* align with enterprise principles;
* strengthen the Enterprise Knowledge Fabric;
* preserve interoperability;
* support long-term maintainability.

Architectural coherence shall take precedence over short-term implementation convenience.

---

# 10. Knowledge Preservation Constraint

Enterprise knowledge is considered a permanent strategic asset.

Knowledge shall be:

* preserved;
* versioned;
* traceable;
* linkable;
* searchable;
* reusable.

Knowledge shall never be intentionally discarded while it retains potential future value.

---

# 11. Review

Strategic assumptions shall be reviewed periodically as the enterprise matures.

Constraints may evolve only through formal architectural governance and only when consistent with the enterprise mission, vision and principles.
