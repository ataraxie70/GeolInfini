# 1. Purpose

This document identifies the stakeholders of Project Atlas and defines their roles, expectations and contributions within the enterprise ecosystem.

Unlike traditional stakeholder models, Project Atlas recognizes that stakeholders not only consume value but also contribute to the continuous creation, validation and enrichment of enterprise knowledge.

---

# 2. Stakeholder Philosophy

Every stakeholder participates in one or more enterprise value cycles.

Stakeholders may simultaneously consume knowledge, contribute expertise, support decisions and strengthen the community.

Knowledge creation is therefore a collective responsibility.

---

# 3. Stakeholder Categories

The enterprise recognizes four primary stakeholder families.

* Knowledge Producers
* Knowledge Consumers
* Ecosystem Facilitators
* Enterprise Governance

---

# 4. Knowledge Producers

Knowledge Producers create and maintain enterprise knowledge assets.

Typical stakeholders include:

* Atlas content creators
* Technical experts
* Product specialists
* Trainers
* Reviewers
* Authors
* Technicians
* Sales advisors

Primary contributions:

* articles
* videos
* tutorials
* product presentations
* comparisons
* buying guides
* technical documentation

---

# 5. Knowledge Consumers

Knowledge Consumers use Atlas to improve technology decisions.

Representative profiles include:

* students
* parents
* software developers
* gamers
* architects and CAD professionals
* graphic designers
* video creators
* entrepreneurs
* SMEs
* public institutions
* NGOs
* teachers
* researchers
* beginners
* technology enthusiasts

Primary expectations:

* trusted information
* understandable explanations
* personalized recommendations
* long-term support

---

# 6. Ecosystem Facilitators

Facilitators extend enterprise value beyond the platform.

Representative stakeholders include:

* suppliers
* distributors
* repair centers
* logistics providers
* educational institutions
* training organizations
* strategic partners
* technology vendors

Primary contributions:

* products
* services
* expertise
* operational support

---

# 7. Enterprise Governance

Governance stakeholders ensure quality, consistency and sustainability.

Representative roles include:

* Enterprise Architects
* Knowledge Curators
* Community Moderators
* Knowledge Managers
* Content Editors
* Platform Administrators
* Quality Managers

Primary responsibilities:

* maintain knowledge quality
* preserve conceptual consistency
* enforce enterprise principles
* supervise governance processes
* coordinate enterprise evolution

---

# 8. Stakeholder Relationships

Stakeholders interact through the Enterprise Knowledge Fabric.

Knowledge flows continuously between producers, consumers, facilitators and governance.

Every validated interaction may contribute to improving enterprise knowledge.

---

# 9. Shared Responsibilities

All stakeholders share responsibility for:

* knowledge quality;
* respectful collaboration;
* transparency;
* continuous learning;
* community growth;
* preservation of digital knowledge.

---

# 10. Strategic Perspective

Project Atlas does not consider stakeholders as isolated participants.

Each stakeholder is an active component of a continuously evolving knowledge ecosystem.

The value of the enterprise increases as collaboration, knowledge quality and community participation improve over time.
